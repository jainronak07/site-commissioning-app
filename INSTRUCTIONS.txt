## 🚀 How to Run Locally

### Prerequisites
Ensure you have the following installed on your local machine:
* [Node.js](https://nodejs.org/) (v18 or higher)
* [Git](https://git-scm.com/)
* **Expo Go** app installed on your physical iOS/Android device, or an iOS Simulator / Android Emulator configured.

### Installation & Setup

1. **Clone the repository:**
   ```bash
   git clone [https://github.com/YOUR_USERNAME/site-commissioning-app.git](https://github.com/YOUR_USERNAME/site-commissioning-app.git)

2)Navigate to the project directory:
Bash->   cd site-commissioning-app


3) install dependencies -> npm install

start the dev server 
-> npx expo start -c


||Running the App via Expo Go||
To test the app on a physical device, follow these steps:

Download the Expo Go app from the iOS App Store or Google Play Store on your physical device.

⚠️ Important: Ensure your phone and your computer are connected to the exact same Wi-Fi network.

Scan the QR code generated in your computer's terminal:

iOS: Open the default Camera app, point it at the QR code, and tap the Expo Go prompt that appears.

Android: Open the Expo Go app directly, tap "Scan QR Code", and point it at your screen.

App is live
